Send the same transactional email to many recipients with `emails.batch`, one email each, from a template made in PostHog with `template_data` per recipient, or from content your code rendered. The client sends at most 100 recipients in one request, refusing more before anything is sent, and returns a result per recipient in order: `queued`, `failed` or `unconfirmed`. Each recipient has its own `idempotency_key`, given or made by the client, so the client's own retries never send an email twice. To rerun a batch that was cut off, pass the keys you stored: a rerun with the same keys within 24 hours replays the emails that went out. A request that fails as a whole raises `EmailBatchError` with every recipient's result, such as a 413 `content_too_large` when PostHog finds the batch too large to take at once, with PostHog's fix to split it into smaller batches, or, for a batch of one recipient, the advice to make the email smaller. A recipient that any attempt may have queued stays `unconfirmed`, also when a retry is refused. A failed batch never calls the logger, and a logger that raises while logging an accepted batch's warnings doesn't lose its results.
